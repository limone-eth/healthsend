# H-36 — De-identification is built, proven, and never runs

## Story
- `lib/import.ts` and `lib/deident.ts` are exercised by exactly one caller: `scripts/deident-proof.mjs`.
  The app does not call them. A health record uploaded through the compose screen today reaches
  Swarm with its name and date of birth intact.

## Context
Found while folding H-28 (2026-09-12). Not a defect in H-16 or H-28 — both did what they were
briefed to do, and did it well. The gap is that nothing joined their work to the product.

`lib/sends.ts`'s `createSend` reads each file with `file.arrayBuffer()` straight into
`packEnvelope`. There is no import step, no de-identification, no review. A grep for `deident` or
`importDocument` across `app/` and `lib/sends.ts` returns nothing.

So the landing page's claim — *"Your name and date of birth are set aside as they come in, so they
are never part of anything you send"* — is not merely bounded to labelled patterns, which is what
H-28 was filed for. It is **false in the ordinary case**, for every send the product can currently
make.

`verify:deident` passes 10 fixtures. All ten go through `importDocument` directly. The proof is
sound; it just proves something the product does not do.

## The pattern worth naming
This is the third capability to land proven-but-unwired today, after **End access now** and the
**access log** (both H-31). The backend-slice dispatch pattern is what produced it: while the design
kit was being built, backend stories were the only ones that could run without collisions, so four
of them shipped complete-and-disconnected. That was the right call at the time and it has a debt
attached, which is this story and H-31.

A capability with a passing proof and no caller reads as *done* on the board. It should not.

## What to build
Route uploads through `importDocument` before they reach `packEnvelope`, so identifiers are set
aside before anything is encrypted — that ordering is the whole point of H-16 and must not become
a filter on the way out.

Thread the sender's account name through, which H-28 added as an optional `ImportParams` field with
no caller for exactly this reason.

Surface what was set aside to the sender. `DESIGN.md` § Words we use: *"Your name and date of birth
stay with you"*. The full review screen is H-15; this story needs only enough that the sender is
not told something was removed without being shown what.

**Evidence must be end-to-end**, not another unit proof. Upload a fixture carrying a name through
the real compose flow, fetch the resulting blob from Swarm, decrypt it, and assert the name is
absent. That is the only test that would have caught this.

## Until it lands
`components/demo-notice.tsx` must keep warning that real records should not be uploaded, and must
not imply de-identification is running. H-28 rewrote that copy on the assumption the path was
live — re-check it.
