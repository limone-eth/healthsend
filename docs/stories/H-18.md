# H-18 — 2.7 Remove data

## Story
- Deleting a record while shares depend on it is the sharpest screen in the product: what goes,
  who still has it, and whether their access ends too.

## Context
Depends on: H-13, H-11.
Design: `gbReU` (sheet — no separate mobile frame). `DESIGN.md` § Removing data.

Capped at 560 and centred on desktop, full-bleed and bottom-anchored on a phone. One component,
two placements — drawing two of each would invite them to drift apart.

**End-them-too is a ticked default**, and the sheet has to be straight about the limit: ending a
share stops future access, and a recipient who already read the record keeps what they read. Say
it on the sheet, not in a footnote.

## Redrafted for PDFs, 2026-09-12 — this section wins where the Context above disagrees

The operator narrowed the product to blood test PDFs, uploaded once and bundled per share (H-62/H-63/
H-64/H-66). Resumed from pause on the operator's go-ahead. **The frame is now `i90sl`** ("2.7p Remove a
PDF · sheet"), a PDF copy of `gbReU`, with identical layout and new copy. `gbReU` is kept for reference;
build from `i90sl`.

What that model changes:
- **The thing removed is one PDF**, a `DocumentRecord` in the sender's sealed archive. Removing it means
  resealing the archive without it and moving the archive feed to the new version. **Don't claim the
  bytes are erased.** Earlier archive versions stay in Swarm storage, sealed under the archive key, and
  the footnote says exactly that.
- **"Who still has it" needs a record the build does not keep yet.** A share's bundle is sealed under
  its own share key, so the sender cannot look inside a share to see what it holds. Add a **share index
  inside the sealed archive**: for each share created from the archive, record `{ entityKey,
  documentIds, createdAt, expiresAt }`. `createSendFromArchive` (H-64, `lib/sends.ts`) writes it after
  the grant and key hand-off succeed. It lives under the archive key, never in Arkiv attributes and
  never in anything a recipient can read.
- **Shares have no names.** Recipient labels are blinded and nothing reads them back, and the operator
  settled "no personal name anywhere". Rows read *"Share of 2 documents"* / *"Share of this PDF"* plus
  the existing countdown meta *"Ends 4 December · 74 days"*.
- **Ending a share ends everything in it.** One link is one key and one deadline. The chalk card's copy
  says so, and the checkbox ends every open share that includes the document, using the existing
  `endSend` path plus `markEndedByYou` (see `app/(sender)/shares/local-history.ts`).
- **"Opened on"** comes from the holder access log (`lib/access-log.ts`, `/api/holder/access-log`).
  Show the note only when a listed share has a reliable open. Otherwise omit it; never render a guessed
  date.
- **Only live shares are listed.** A share already ended, expired or missing from `listMySends` is
  left out. If none are live, drop the chalk card and checkbox entirely; the sheet becomes title, item,
  actions and footnote.
- **Shares made before the index existed can't be checked.** If the archive has no index entries but
  `listMySends` returns live archive shares, say so plainly in the chalk card rather than implying the
  PDF is in none. Write the sentence and record it under `## Choices`.
- **Entry point:** the archive list row (H-66) gets a remove control that opens this sheet. After
  removal the row disappears, the eyebrow count drops, and a reload confirms it.

## Reading the design

**`healthsend.pen` is the source. A screenshot of it is not.** Read the nodes with the
`mcp__pencil__execute` tool against `<repo>/healthsend.pen`:

```js
Print(JSON.stringify(Get("<a frame id named in this story>", {depth: 6}), null, 1))
```

- `Get(id, {depth: n})` returns exact geometry, fills, strokes, corner radii, gaps, padding, font
  family, size and weight — and the `$token` references **by name**. None of that survives a
  screenshot, and guessing it is how a build ends up approximately right and visibly off.
- Add `resolveInstances: true` to expand component instances; the ids that come back are
  `instanceId/childId` paths you can read further.
- `Get(id, (n, c) => Print(n.name, c.bounds.width, c.bounds.x))` gives resolved bounds in the
  parent's coordinate space. Use it to check alignment and spacing rather than measuring pixels by
  eye. `c.problems` flags anything clipped by its parent.
- **Copy every string verbatim from the `content` fields.** Re-typing copy from an image is how a
  claim that was deliberately corrected gets silently restored — that has already happened once in
  this project, which is why H-20 says so explicitly.
- `TakeScreenshot([id])` is for confirming your build matches after you have read the nodes. It is
  never the input.

**Read only.** Never call `Insert`, `Update`, `Copy`, `Replace`, `Delete`, `Move`, `SetVariables`
or `Generate` against the canvas, and never commit `healthsend.pen`. The pencil MCP server drives
the operator's live editor, not your worktree's copy of the file — a write from a worker lands in
their open document. The design is an input to this story and an output of none.

Node ids named in this story are stable and can be read directly. The frame is the contract; where
this story's prose and the frame disagree, read the frame and say so in `## Choices`.

**Routing:** this story needs a worker whose engine has the `pencil` MCP server. It is configured
globally on this machine, so a Claude worker in a worktree inherits it; an engine without it can
only see exported images and must not be given this story.


## Review log

## Choices

- **H-64's `createSendFromArchive` is still on its own branch, not on `main`.** Everything
  in step 1 is done: the share index (`ShareIndexEntry`, `lib/archive.ts`), `removeDocument`,
  `addShareIndexEntry`, the pure `sharesIncludingDocument`, their Swarm-backed counterparts
  in `lib/archive-store.ts`, and `RemoveDocumentSheet` with its logic module and proof
  script. Step 2's list-row entry point is wired (`app/(sender)/page.tsx`'s remove control,
  `app/(sender)/live-shares-for-document.ts`). The one piece left is the other end of that
  wiring: once `createSendFromArchive` lands, it must call `addShareIndexEntryToMyArchive`
  (`lib/archive-store.ts`) after its grant and key hand-off both succeed, the same way
  `createSend` already calls the holder only once the grant exists. No code in this story
  needs to change for that — the function is written and proved; it only needs a caller.
- **`indexUnknown` is wired but always computed `false` for now.** The "shares made before
  the index existed" case needs a way to recognise a live grant as "from this archive"
  without an index entry, and nothing in today's `Grant` shape (`lib/arkiv.ts`) offers that
  signal — it does not exist as a concept until `createSendFromArchive` defines what an
  archive share looks like on Arkiv. `loadLiveSharesForDocument`
  (`app/(sender)/live-shares-for-document.ts`) says so in a comment at the point that matters.
  Once H-64 lands, revisit that one `const indexUnknown = false` with whatever signal it
  actually offers.
- **The chalk card's "some shares can't be checked" sentence is new copy**, not from the
  frame — `i90sl` has no state for this, since it predates the index existing at all. Wrote:
  "This archive has no record of shares made before this list existed. If an older share
  still includes this PDF, end it from Your shares instead." (`UNKNOWN_SHARES_NOTE`,
  `components/remove-document-sheet-logic.ts`).
- **The singular "opened" note ("It was opened on…") is my own extrapolation.** The frame's
  one example always has two shares ("One of these shares was opened on…"); a sheet with
  exactly one live share needed its own grammar, so `openedNoteText` picks `"It"` for a
  single share and names the earliest reliable open when more than one share qualifies.
- **The share row wraps onto two lines below the frame's 560px width, and stays one line at
  it.** Checking the sheet in a real browser at 400px (required by this story) showed
  `i90sl`'s single-line "label + ends-date" row truncating "Share of 2 documents" mid-word
  once a real phone's full-bleed width (well under 560, even edge-to-edge) squeezed it
  against the date. `flex-wrap` on that row keeps the frame's exact layout at 560px/desktop
  and reflows the date onto its own line only when the label needs the room.
- **The remove control on each document row is a plain icon button with no frame to read
  from.** H-66's list (`app/(sender)/page.tsx`) shipped before this story and has no
  share-status pill or remove affordance yet; a bare `Trash` icon button, right-aligned on
  each row, is a reasonable placeholder short of a dedicated frame — one was not named for
  this entry point in the story or in `i90sl`.
- **`endsLabel`, `sharesHeadline`, `endAllCheckboxLabel` and `shareLabel` are new pure
  helpers** in `components/remove-document-sheet-logic.ts` rather than reused from
  `components/ui.tsx`'s `Countdown` — the frame's "Ends 4 December · 74 days" format differs
  from `Countdown`'s "Expires 4 December 2026" / "N days left" pair, and generalising
  `Countdown` to cover both looked like more risk to an already-shipped primitive than
  writing four small, independently-tested functions.
