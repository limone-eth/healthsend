# FAQ

*The questions every hacker asks, answered in one place. If it is not here, ask us directly: hello@urbe.build or the Telegram group.*

## Before you arrive

*What ETHRome is, who gets in, and what it costs. Short answer: nothing, if you were selected.*

### What is ETHRome?

ETHRome is an international hackathon for web3 builders, now at its 4th edition. It runs from Friday 11 to Sunday 13 September 2026 at Urbe Hub in Testaccio, Rome. 40 selected builders, focused on AI, Finance and Privacy for web3.

### What should I build?

Anything you want. AI for web3, Finance for web3 and Privacy for web3 are the themes of the edition, and the areas where our sponsors and mentors are strongest, so building near them tends to pay off. You are free to work on one of them, across all three, or somewhere else entirely. One single ranking, one winning team.

### Who can attend?

Builders of all backgrounds: developers, designers and other skill sets. Applications are evaluated on interest and experience in the Ethereum ecosystem and on demonstrated ability to build. Participation is by selection: only builders who received an acceptance message can take part.

### How much does it cost?

Nothing. ETHRome is free for approved hackers, and meals, drinks and snacks are included for the whole weekend.

### Can I participate remotely?

No. ETHRome is an IRL hackathon and remote hacking is not allowed: every member of your team has to be physically at Urbe Hub, both to build and to present the submission to the judges. You declared this when you registered.

### How big can a team be?

Maximum 3 people. You can also hack solo.

### I am under 18, can I take part?

Only with written authorization from both parents or legal guardians, accompanied by the identity documents of the signatories. You must also be accompanied by an authorized adult for the whole duration of the hackathon.

## During the hackathon

*What happens between check-in on Friday and the submission deadline on Sunday morning.*

### Is there a conference or side events?

ETHRome is a hackathon: the weekend is built around 40 hours of building. There are two short sponsor workshops on Friday evening, Arkiv at 19:00 and Team1 at 19:30, twenty minutes each. Everything else on the [Schedule](schedule.md) page is hacking, meals and ceremonies.

### Are pre-existing projects allowed?

Yes, under three conditions, all of them mandatory:

- You state it explicitly in your submission.
- You build new functionality or extensions, and only that new part is evaluated.
- You work in your existing repository, made public: no fresh repo with a single commit of old code. Only commits made after the start of the hackathon count.

Miss one and the team is excluded from the competition. Full detail on the [Rules](rules.md) page.

### When and where do I submit?

Through a single **Google Form**. Deadline: Sunday 13 September at 10:00 (Europe/Rome). Everything the form asks is on the [Submit](submissions.md) page.

### Can I give this manual to my AI agent?

Yes, that is why it also exists in Markdown. The repo [urbeETH/ethrome-2026-hacker-manual](https://github.com/urbeETH/ethrome-2026-hacker-manual) mirrors these pages: clone it next to your project, add it as a submodule, or point your tool at the raw single file version. Pull before each session, the repo is refreshed when the site changes. If the two ever disagree, the site wins.

### Where do I ask something that is not here?

Write to [hello@urbe.build](mailto:hello@urbe.build) or ask in the [Telegram group](https://t.me/+wpq6ea2etywwYzI0). During the weekend, any organizer on site can help.

## Judging and prizes

*One prize from ETHRome, bounties from the sponsors. Two separate things, judged by different people.*

### How does judging work?

To be judged you need the minimum requirements: open source code, contract addresses if you deployed, and a demo video of maximum 3 minutes. Then you present your project to a group of judges and answer their questions. How long each slot lasts is decided on the day and announced during the event. The full criteria are on the [Judging](judging.md) page.

### What do I win?

The winning team wins one year of Urbe Hub membership for every member, up to 3 people. On top of that, whatever bounties the sponsors decide to award: $5,000 is on the table.

### Who are the sponsors?

Arkiv, Team1, Swarm and ENS. See the [Prizes](prizes.md) page for what they bring. Open a sponsor there to read what it wants you to build.

### What are the bounties?

Bounties are put up, chosen and paid by the sponsors alone: ETHRome has no say in the selection and does not handle delivery in any way. All four sponsors have confirmed their amount: **Arkiv $2,500**, **Team1 $1,000**, **Swarm $1,000** and **ENS $500**, for a pool of **$5,000**. What each bounty asks for sits inside its own block on the [Prizes](prizes.md) page: click a sponsor and it opens. All four briefs are there in full.

### Can I apply to more than one bounty?

Yes. Apply to as many bounties as genuinely fit what you built, by ticking them in the submission form. Going after a bounty does not affect how the ETHRome judges score you.

**Team1 is the one exception, twice over.** Inside their bounty you enter one track only, Track A or Track B, and the form asks you which. They also run their own submission form on top of ours, so for that bounty you fill in both. Details in the Team1 block on the [Prizes](prizes.md) page.

**Arkiv works the other way round:** you can go for one, two or all three of their missions, but a team wins one Arkiv prize at most. Their block has the rest.

### How do I get paid if I win a bounty?

By the sponsor, directly. ETHRome does not distribute bounty prizes and does not run KYC on bounty winners: each sponsor pays its own winners and handles any checks on its own side. Each sponsor tells its winners how and when, after the event. Nothing about the payout changes what you build, so do not let it slow you down.
