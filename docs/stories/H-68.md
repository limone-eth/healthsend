# H-68 — Hide the Assistant tab for the PDF proof of concept

## Story
- Someone using the PDF-only build sees two destinations, Your archive and Your shares. Nothing offers
  an assistant that cannot read what their archive holds.

## Why
Operator decision, 2026-09-12: **hide the tab for now, keep the code.** The assistant (H-22) connects
to blood-panel and wearable-series records over MCP. The proof of concept narrowed to PDFs only
(H-62/H-63/H-64/H-66), and `app/assistant/page.tsx` says itself that documents are not offered. In a
PDF-only archive the tab has nothing to connect, so in a demo it reads as broken.

This is a hide, not a removal. H-22's work comes back when the assistant learns PDFs.

## What changes
- **Rail and mobile tab bar:** drop the `assistant` destination from what `components/chrome.tsx`
  renders. Prefer one switch, such as a constant next to `DESTINATIONS` with a comment pointing here,
  over deleting the entry. Bringing the tab back should be a one-line change. Keep the
  `SenderDestination` type compiling for `app/assistant/page.tsx`.
- **`/assistant` stays built and reachable by URL.** Do not delete the page, `app/api/mcp/**`,
  `lib/mcp*.ts` or their tests. Nothing in the sender UI links to it.
- **User-facing claims:** any copy that promises an assistant or MCP connector to someone using the
  app, such as the landing page, must not promise a feature they cannot reach. Remove or park those
  sentences. Where the copy sits in a file another live worker owns, list the file and line under
  `## Choices` instead of editing it; the orchestrator lands those at fold.
- **Tests:** update chrome and e2e specs that count or click three destinations. Add a test that fails
  if the Assistant destination is rendered.

## Evidence
Browser at 1440 and 400: the rail and tab bar show two destinations, clicking each works, and there is
zero horizontal overflow. `/assistant` loaded directly still renders. `pnpm lint`, `pnpm build`,
`pnpm exec tsc --noEmit`, `pnpm test` and every `verify:*` lane pass.
