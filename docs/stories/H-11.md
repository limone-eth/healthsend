# H-11 — End access now

## Story
- `deleteShare` exists in `lib/holder-store.ts` and is wired to nothing. The design promises a
  sender can end a share before its date, and today she cannot.

## Context
Depends on: H-4. Blocks: H-10, H-18, and one line of H-9.

This is the capability the split-key architecture bought, and it is worth being precise about why
it works: deleting the holder's half makes the link unjoinable. Before the split, "revoke" would
have been a promise to stop showing something that was still published.

**Authorisation is the whole story.** Only the grant's owner may end it. Prove it with a signature
from the sender's derived Arkiv key over the entity key and a timestamp — not a bearer secret
returned at create time, which leaks into logs and browser history and would let anyone who saw it
end someone else's share.

- `DESIGN.md` § Words we use: the control says **End access now**, never "Revoke". *Revoke is a
  permissions word from software she resents. Ending something is what she is actually doing.*
- The confirm sheet needs a design stage — it is destructive and irreversible.
- Sets the `Ended by you` state: *"She lost access the moment you tapped it. Nothing to negotiate
  and nobody to ask."*
- Be honest in the copy about the limit that no design fixes: a recipient who already opened the
  document keeps what their browser received.
