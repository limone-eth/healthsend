# H-48 — Your shares reports the wrong thing about what has ended

## Story
- A live share can be shown as ended because of a query limit, a refused revoke can lock the sheet,
  and the history of what ended lives in one browser.

## Context
From review 2.

**R2-008 — high — the 50-row grant cap marks live shares as ended.** `lib/arkiv.ts:228` caps the
query, and a sender past that cap sees live shares reported as ended. This is the worst kind of
wrong answer in this product: it tells someone access has stopped when it has not, so they stop
worrying about a link that still opens.

**R2-013 — medium — a rejected revoke locks the confirmation sheet.**
`app/(sender)/shares/page.tsx:113`. "End access now" is the one destructive control here, and a
sheet that cannot be dismissed or retried after a refusal leaves the sender unsure whether it
happened.

**R2-014 — medium — ended-share history exists only in one browser.**
`app/(sender)/shares/local-history.ts:23`. The same passkey on another device shows a different
history, which contradicts what sign-in tells the sender: the same face opens the same archive.

## What to build
- Page the grant query, or make the cap visible. A sender must never be told a live share ended.
  If the honest fix is "we cannot list more than N and we say so", that is acceptable and the
  guessing is not.
- A refused revoke returns the sheet to a state the sender can act from, and says what happened.
- Decide where ended-share history lives and make sign-in's promise true, or change the promise.
  Say which you chose and why in `## Choices` — this one is a design call, not only a bug.

## Evidence
A sender with more than 50 grants, and a holder that refuses a revoke. Both as tests that fail
before the fix.
