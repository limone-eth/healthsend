# H-16 — De-identification at import

## Story
- The landing page already tells people *"Your name and date of birth are set aside as they come
  in, so they are never part of anything you send."* Nothing does this. Either build it or stop
  claiming it.

## Context
Depends on: H-13. Related: H-23, which is the honest stopgap while this is unbuilt.
Brief §8. `DESIGN.md` § Words we use.

This is the main safety story on the board. A lab PDF carries a name, a date of birth, often an
address and a patient number, and a share scoped to "five markers" that quietly ships the header
block has shared far more than the sender chose.

**Never use the word "anonymous", and do not let the implementation imply it.** `DESIGN.md` is
explicit that health data re-identifies and that claiming otherwise is the one promise we would
actually be caught out on. The copy is *"Your name and date of birth stay with you"* — a statement
about what we set aside, not a claim about what can be inferred from what remains.

Strip at import, before anything is encrypted, so the identifiers never reach the archive rather
than being filtered on the way out.
