## What to build

### F2 — high — Archived selection cannot advance on mobile
`app/(sender)/new/page.tsx`: the mobile step-1 **Continue** checks only `selectedFiles.length`. Enable
it when either fresh files **or** archived documents are selected, matching the summary line the
sender already sees.

### F6 — high — A partial share-end failure is silently discarded
`app/(sender)/page.tsx`: `performRemoveDocument` can return `removed-partial` with `failedShares`, but
the page handles only `refused`.

On `removed-partial`:
- Keep the result visible. The PDF is removed, so reflect that in the list.
- Tell the sender, in plain words, that some shares are still open. Give each one's end label, and
  point to Your shares to end it there.
- Never close as if everything ended.

Suggested copy: *"Removed from your archive. 1 share could not be ended and is still open — end it from
Your shares."* Record the final wording under `## Choices`.

### F7 — medium — A long remove sheet hides its context on mobile
`components/remove-document-sheet.tsx`: the sheet has no height limit or inner scroll.
- Cap the sheet at the viewport, accounting for the safe area.
- Scroll the share list inside it.
- Keep the title, the PDF identity, and the Remove / Keep actions reachable at 400×800 with 12 live
  shares.

### F8 — medium — Archive rows omit a known share count
`app/(sender)/page.tsx` rows (desktop and mobile).
- The share index now exists (H-18 / H-70). When a row's document is in one or more **live, indexed**
  shares, show an "In N shares" pill, matching the frame's pill style.
- Compute it from the archive's `shareIndex` plus the live share list, as the remove sheet does
  (`app/(sender)/live-shares-for-document.ts`). Reuse that code; don't add a second path.
- When unknown (live unindexed shares exist) or the list hasn't loaded, show **no** pill. Never show
  "Not shared".
- Keep the page fast: one live-share fetch for the whole page, not one per row.

### F9 — medium — The landing page still promises the hidden assistant
`app/landing/page.tsx:131`, `:135` ("…a clinician or your assistant…") and `:171` ("An assistant you use
daily"). H-68 hid the assistant tab and removed the dedicated section but left these claims.
- Remove the assistant from the hero line.
- Replace or remove the third problem card, keeping the grid balanced.
- Every visible claim must be true of the build.
