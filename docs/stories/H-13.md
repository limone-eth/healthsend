# H-13 — The archive data model

## Story
- The design shares **records** — 32 markers of which 5 are shared, 28 nights of sleep, per-metric
  scope inside Wearables. The app shares **files**. That is not a styling gap; it is a different
  product underneath, and six screens are waiting on it.

## Context
Depends on: H-4. **Blocks H-14, H-15, H-16, H-17, H-18, H-19** and the deferred half of H-5.

`lib/envelope.ts` packs raw files into one blob under one key. The design needs:

- A **record model** — a blood panel is 32 markers with values, units and reference ranges; a
  wearable export is a nightly series. `DESIGN.md` § Where the extracted values actually live.
- An **archive** held encrypted at rest, which the sender adds to over time rather than uploading
  per send.
- **Addressing**, so a share can scope to a subset without re-encrypting the whole archive and
  without the scope itself leaking. Arkiv attributes are publicly queryable; sensitive values are
  HMAC'd under a user-held key, only timestamps left plaintext for range queries.

Two constraints from the existing code, both learned the hard way and both in `CLAUDE.md`: Arkiv
attribute names are lowercase — the `Ident32` grammar rejects `A`–`Z` *on-chain*, so `createdAt`
costs a reverted transaction — and query predicates must carry the same tagged type as the write,
or they silently match nothing.

`DESIGN.md` § The demo dataset fixes every figure the screens cross-check each other on. Build the
model so those numbers come out of it rather than being typed into components.
