# Schedule

*Three days, one screen. Friday 11 to Sunday 13 September at Urbe Hub. Screenshot this page.*

## What matters

*Four things to keep in your head. Everything else is detail.*

| Value | Detail |
|---|---|
| **40 hours** | Hacking window, Fri 18:00 to Sun 10:00 |
| **24/7** | Venue access during the hackathon |
| **Sun 10:00** | Submission deadline, no extensions |
| **Sun 16:00** | Closing, everyone out standing |

All times are Europe/Rome (CEST).

### Friday 11 September

*Arrive, check in, start building. The clock starts before the ceremony, not after it.*

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 16:00 | **Doors open, check-in** | Come early, get your badge, claim a desk. |
| 16:30 | Sponsor desks open | Swarm hands out gift codes that cover storage for the whole weekend. Pick one up now and you never touch a token to build on them. |
| 18:00 | **Hacking starts** | The 40-hour clock starts here. Sharp. |
| 18:30 | **Opening ceremony** | Format, rules, themes, sponsor bounties, plus a short keynote by Arkiv. Stop typing for this one, it is where the bounties get explained. |
| 19:00 | Workshop: Arkiv | From Postgres to Arkiv: web2 to web3 database in 20 minutes. Santiago Trujillo Zuluaga, live build, 20 minutes. |
| 19:30 | Workshop: Team1 | Build the Proof, Not the Pitch: shipping a verifiable Avalanche project. Giacomo Barbieri, 20 minutes. |
| 20:00 | Dinner | At the venue, included. |
| 00:00 | Midnight Roman street food | A late refuel, on us. |

### Saturday 12 September

*Full hacking day. Venue open 24/7, come and go as you need.*

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Breakfast | Fuel up before the long stretch. |
| 13:00 | Lunch | Step away from the keyboard for twenty minutes. |
| 20:00 | Dinner, and the Arkiv cut-off | Last full meal before the final push. Going for Arkiv? Your friction report and the ten-minute conversation with their team close now, not on Sunday. |
| 00:00 | Midnight Roman street food | The one that carries you to sunrise. |

### Sunday 13 September

*Ship it, demo it, celebrate it.*

| Time | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Breakfast | The last hour is the fastest. Eat first. |
| 10:00 | **Submission deadline** | Hands off the keyboard. Late entries do not count. |
| 10:30 | Judging session | Project reviews plus sponsor bounty reviews. |
| 13:00 | Lunch | Breathe. The hard part is done. |
| 15:00 | **Closing ceremony and awards** | Demos on stage, then the overall winner and the sponsor bounties. |
| 16:00 | End | See you at the next one. |

> 🛠️ **Both Friday workshops are confirmed.** Arkiv at 19:00 migrates a Postgres database to Arkiv live, and leaves you with the schema file their bounty asks for. Team1 at 19:30 walks through turning a hackathon idea into a build you can prove, plus their bounty requirements. Twenty minutes each, and the cheapest forty minutes of your weekend if you are going for either bounty. This schedule is locked.

## How the weekend works

The venue stays open around the clock from Friday doors to Sunday closing. Sleep when you want, hack when you want, nobody will kick you out at midnight. Meals are included for approved hackers, so plan around the meal times above instead of hunting for food in Testaccio at 3 AM.

The two deadlines with teeth are Sunday 10:00 (submissions close) and Sunday 15:00 (awards). Everything in between is judging, so keep your team reachable on Sunday morning.
